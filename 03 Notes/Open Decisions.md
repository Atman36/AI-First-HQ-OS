# Open Decisions

## 2026-04-15

### 1. External Write Policy

- Question: which systems may AI write to in Stage 2: email, calendar, CRM, docs, code, or deployments?
- Why unresolved: the policy should reflect real business risk, not generic best practice.
- Needed from: CEO and Governor

### 2. Budget Envelope For Autonomous Actions

- Question: what pre-approved spend, if any, may an agent commit without a fresh human approval?
- Current default: zero unbudgeted spend.
- Needed from: CEO and Finance

### 3. Customer-Facing Autonomy

- Question: may agents ever send external messages without human review, and in which workflows?
- Current default: no external sends without review.
- Needed from: CEO, Governor, Growth

### 4. Deployment Authority

- Question: may Delivery ever ship production-affecting changes autonomously, or only prepare PRs and deployment plans?
- Current default: no production deployment autonomy.
- Needed from: CEO and Delivery

### 5. Memory Retention Boundary

- Question: which runtime memories should remain in `.hq/` and which should move to product or system-of-record layers later?
- Why unresolved: premature memory expansion would create noise and privacy risk.
- Needed from: CEO and Governor
