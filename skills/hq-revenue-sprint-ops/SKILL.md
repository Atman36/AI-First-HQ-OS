## Default Workflow

1. Keep the corridor narrow: growth-stage identity, fraud, or data infrastructure SaaS moving upmarket.
2. Use only public facts for account-fit sanity checks unless the founder provides private data.
3. Keep account-specific raw research, draft packets, and buyer notes under `.hq/`.
4. Draft outputs for founder review, not direct send: LinkedIn copy, email copy, discovery prompts, qualification notes, trust-ceiling language, and objection notes.
5. Separate facts, assumptions, and founder-only wording decisions.
6. Require founder approval before any outbound email, LinkedIn message, form submission, call request, buyer-facing attachment, public publish, or trust/legal claim.
7. Capture anonymized learnings into the approved update surface only after the founder accepts the conclusion.

## Guardrails

- Do not broaden ICP as the first fix when outreach underperforms.
- Do not invent customer proof, compliance status, legal claims, or buyer facts.
- Do not commit prospect-specific research or private outreach drafts.
- Keep the success signal tied to real discovery response, live questionnaire evidence, or concrete buyer objection.
