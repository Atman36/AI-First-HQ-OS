## Why This Project Exists

- The founder already has multiple real assets, but they are not yet organized into a deliberate money-making sequence.
- The company needs first revenue proof before adding more product surface area.
- HQ should become the management layer that turns portfolio sprawl into a commercial operating system.

## Portfolio Snapshot

- [`consultant-execution`](</Users/Apple/Developer/consultant-execution/README.md>): execution-management and meeting-review SaaS MVP with workspace model, auth, weekly planning, and Zoom review flow.
- [`v0-sales-os-ai-prototype`](</Users/Apple/Developer/v0-sales-os-ai-prototype/README.md>): sales-ops and call-processing stack with stronger upside but heavier integration and onboarding complexity.
- [`tg-hr-bot`](</Users/Apple/Developer/tg-hr-bot/README.md>): early HR-bot skeleton; not the first monetization candidate.
- `Krylatye Kacheli`: unfinished children-events website redesign; useful as a fast cash bridge, portfolio proof, and design case study rather than the main long-term SaaS bet.

## Strategic Thesis

- The first money should come from a productized pilot or redesign offer built on top of existing assets, not from another greenfield build.
- The recommended first commercial wedge is `consultant-execution` because it has the clearest workspace-based story and lower launch complexity than the sales-ops stack.
- `v0-sales-os-ai-prototype` stays as the second commercial track with higher upside after one narrower offer is already sold.
- `tg-hr-bot` stays in reserve until there is either a concrete HR design partner or a stronger operational base.
- `Krylatye Kacheli` is a tactical cash and proof asset: it can generate a redesign case, a testimonial, and possibly the very first paid check faster than a full SaaS sale.

## Revenue Plan

### 0-30 Days

- Sell one paid diagnostic, pilot, or redesign deposit.
- Package one offer that is easy to explain, price, and deliver manually.
- Talk to real prospects before adding more code.
- Keep onboarding human-assisted; do not wait for full self-serve readiness.

### 31-90 Days

- Close 3-5 paid pilots or a smaller number of higher-ticket implementations.
- Use those pilots to decide whether `consultant-execution` or `v0-sales-os-ai-prototype` becomes the main productization bet.
- Turn the winning wedge into repeatable onboarding, proof, and a recurring billing model.

### 91-365 Days

- Converge on one core product with one clear ICP and one primary acquisition story.
- Move from founder-led delivery to repeatable packaging, onboarding, and retained subscription revenue.
- Use services, pilots, and redesign work only as bridges toward a concentrated recurring offer, not as the final business model.

## Recommended First Offers

### Offer A: AI Execution System Setup

- Base asset: `consultant-execution`
- ICP: founders, boutique consultancies, small service firms, and operating teams that already run weekly planning and too many meetings manually
- Delivery model: paid setup plus concierge onboarding and weekly review support
- Reason to test first: fastest path to a narrow value story without deep external integrations

### Offer B: AI Sales Ops Audit And Pilot

- Base asset: `v0-sales-os-ai-prototype`
- ICP: small sales teams with call volume, CRM discipline, and appetite for workflow automation
- Delivery model: audit plus narrow pilot, initially with manual support and restricted integrations
- Reason to test second: higher upside, but more integration risk and longer time to trust

### Offer C: Krylatye Kacheli Relaunch

- Base asset: redesign and commercial packaging for the existing children-events website
- ICP: local events/family entertainment demand and adjacent service work
- Delivery model: redesign package, launch refresh, booking funnel cleanup, and case-study capture
- Reason to keep live: can generate immediate proof and cash while the SaaS wedge is still being validated

## Simple Financial Logic

- First 30 days do not require perfect SaaS pricing; they require one real payment.
- The likely path to the first money is a paid audit, deposit, or guided setup rather than a pure self-serve subscription.
- The path to `$10,000` in 90 days is likely a mix of implementation fees and early recurring retainers, not SaaS MRR alone.
- The path to `~$1M ARR` requires choosing one winning wedge by evidence and turning it into recurring revenue with tighter onboarding, proof, and positioning.

## Team Shape

- CEO: final strategy, pricing approval, sales conversations, high-risk decisions, and offer selection.
- AI Operations Lead: queue health, sequencing, weekly review, and making sure commercial work enters the control plane cleanly.
- Growth: ICP definition, offer packaging, messaging, channel tests, and proof collection.
- Research: market checks, competitor sanity checks, and fact/probable/hypothesis separation.
- Finance: pricing floors, unit economics, billing route options, and cash-risk visibility.
- Delivery: landing pages, demos, onboarding assets, and redesign implementation.
- Governor: guardrails for privacy, public claims, payments, and customer-facing autonomy.
- Documentation: keep shared truth aligned after decisions are accepted.
