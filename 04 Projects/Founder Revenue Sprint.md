## Goal

- Reach the first narrow external revenue wedge without reopening broad portfolio exploration.
- Package one offer that can be sold, delivered manually, and then expanded into recurring revenue.
- Keep accepted management conclusions in tracked truth while leaving raw research and operating artifacts under `.hq/`.

## Why This Project Exists

- HQ needs one focused commercial wedge with real pain and a credible path to repeatable revenue.
- The founder should validate one high-conviction workflow instead of spreading effort across the whole portfolio.
- The accepted conclusion from research needs to be turned into a project brief, decision record, and next-step operating constraints.

## Selected Wedge

- First product direction: `AI Security Questionnaire Automation`
- First paid offer: human-reviewed `Security Questionnaire Deal Velocity Pilot`
- Why this wins first:
  - it sits on a real and already standardized workflow;
  - it ties directly to active enterprise deal friction;
  - it can be delivered manually before platform build-out;
  - it compounds into reusable answer libraries, evidence maps, and later trust operations.

## First ICP

- Primary ICP: growth-stage B2B SaaS companies moving upmarket into mid-market or enterprise deals
- Practical fit:
  - active customer security reviews;
  - baseline security documents already exist;
  - lean security or compliance capacity;
  - live revenue friction from questionnaire turnaround.
- Likely buyers:
  - primary: revenue or deal owner affected by delays;
  - secondary: security, GRC, CTO, or founder sponsor who owns the source materials.

## First Paid Offer

### Security Questionnaire Deal Velocity Pilot

- Format: fixed-scope, manual-first pilot with human review
- Core promise: turn the customer's existing security materials into a faster, more repeatable questionnaire response workflow
- First deliverable:
  - source inventory;
  - answer library v1;
  - evidence map v1;
  - first live questionnaire response draft or completed workflow;
  - gap log for unresolved items.
- Not in scope:
  - broad trust platform;
  - legal vertical product build;
  - deep integrations;
  - autonomous answering without human review;
  - custom one-off engineering for early prospects.

## Challenger

- `Legal Vertical RAG` remains the strongest challenger.
- Status now: parked, low-intensity exploration only.
- Why it waits:
  - the market is real, but the first-revenue path is more credibility-heavy;
  - legal ethics, confidentiality, supervision, and incumbents raise the proof burden;
  - the security wedge offers a cleaner manual-first path to paid work now.

## Strategic Rules

- Keep the offer narrow and tied to live deal velocity.
- Sell the workflow before building the platform.
- Keep human review in the loop for sensitive answers and evidence handling.
- Treat cross-border entity, banking, sanctions, and tax execution as unresolved until human counsel validates the route.
- Import only accepted conclusions into tracked files; keep raw research, prospect lists, customer docs, and private operating artifacts under `.hq/`.
