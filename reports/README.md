# Reports

Reports are inputs to decision-making, not source of truth by themselves.

## Rule

A report changes company state only after its conclusion is summarized into one or more tracked files such as:

- `03 Notes/Decisions.md`
- `03 Notes/Open Decisions.md`
- `04 Projects/*`
- `05 AI Control Plane/*`

## Evidence Standard

When adding a new report, explicitly separate:

- confirmed
- probable
- hypothesis
- open questions

## Suggested Placement

- `reports/deep-research/` for long-form market or architecture research
- keep filenames dated where practical
